# Code of Conduct

VocaTa follows a simple collaboration rule: keep discussions focused, respectful, and
useful for improving the project.

## Expected Behavior

- Be clear about facts, assumptions, and uncertainty.
- Critique code and decisions, not people.
- Keep review comments actionable.
- Respect privacy: do not share secrets, tokens, private logs, or personal data.
- Give enough context for others, including AI coding agents, to reproduce and verify work.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, insults, threats, or personal attacks.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Sharing credentials, production-only configuration, or sensitive logs.
- Repeatedly derailing technical discussions after maintainers ask to refocus.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or hide comments, close issues, reject contributions, or block
participants when behavior harms the project or exposes sensitive information.
